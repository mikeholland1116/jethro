Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

Jethro is a personal assistant used by one person, its owner, Mike Holland. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

Who uses it

Only the owner. Jethro does not offer accounts, sign-ups or access to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Jethro can read and organise the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs. It uses this only to help the owner: summarising, filing, scheduling and drafting.

Where data goes

Data stays in the owner's own Google account and on the owner's own computer. Jethro uses an AI model to read and draft on the owner's behalf; that processing is done for the owner only. Data is never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never used for anything other than helping the owner.

Google user data

Jethro's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Jethro's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
