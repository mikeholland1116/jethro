Jethro

A personal assistant for one person.

Jethro is a private assistant built by Mike Holland for his own use. It helps him keep his personal calendar, email and household admin organised, and reaches him by text message.

It is not a public product. Nobody else can sign in to it or use it, and it has no other users.

Privacy policy

Named for Jethro in Exodus 18, who told Moses he couldn't carry everything alone.
